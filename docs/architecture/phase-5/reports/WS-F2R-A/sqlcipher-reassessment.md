# SQLCipher reassessment

The historical ADR 0004 selects an early SQLite direction. Later OD-R04/05 specify offline SQLite and topology, and OD-R07/08 require encrypted profiles and independent recovery. SQLCipher is an engineering candidate for those outcomes. The later proposed encryption specification also protects sensitive catalog content separately. Re-evaluating the backend does not reopen those requirements. ## Twelve questions

1. **What genuinely requires database-level encryption?** High-value, queryable persistent product records are the strongest fit: history and indexes, bookmarks, session state, downloads, grants, profile config, sensitive catalog and durable lifecycle records. Policy requires the protection outcome for full profiles and separately reviewed sensitive catalog data. It can be met by authenticated storage other than a page codec if the complete leakage, integrity and transaction contract is shown. See [data classification](data-classification.md).
2. **What could use native OS-protected files?** Strictly allowlisted low-sensitivity global preferences may use OS-protected files or ordinary SQLite. Temporary private state stays memory-only; explicit downloads remain user files. Small sensitive envelopes and archives may be native files containing authenticated ciphertext. The platform protection still supplements the app layer. See [ownership boundaries](ownership-boundaries.md).
3. **Ciphertext-only while running?** Sensitive durable bytes, indexes, sidecars, snapshots and staging must stay protected even while the process is running. Authorized computation will handle transient plaintext and keys in trusted memory. Bound lifetime, copies and error paths. No universal guarantee about memory, swap, system crash capture or device state.
4. **Offline filesystem access?** Yes. A copied product store or portable backup should remain confidential and detect tampering without the relevant key. Volume protection under platform conditions is additional defense. Backups/copies may be outside that volume and the original account.
5. **Same-user processes?** Treat hostile local processes, renderer compromise and permitted file access as threats to copied or accessible bytes and broker boundaries. Sandbox/ACLs and scoped, nonexported keys help under those conditions. Same-user access to a compromised trusted process, credentials or OS is outside the promised protection. Exercise independently signed access-denial and copy/tamper cases.
6. **Logout, lock and termination?** Yes for persisted confidentiality and next-access gating. At application-profile lock, fence admission, retire views, close or quarantine handles, drain/cancel stale work and invalidate leases. At logout/termination re-establish authority on relaunch; no assumption of physical memory erasure. Sleep/session transition and actual screen-lock coverage are separate gates.
7. **What do Keychain and native file protection provide?** Keychain controls secret access under identity, ACL and OS conditions. FileVault protects storage at the volume boundary. OS container/file classes constrain access under their specific rules. None implements Vezham's product database encryption, authenticated record identity, cross-Mac recovery or profile lock automatically. [Native inventory](ownership-boundaries.md)
8. **What needs an encrypted database or equivalent?** Efficient persistent searchable records with protected page/index content, authenticated reads and transactional updates favor a qualified page codec. SQLCipher provides designed page encryption and MACs; application-level identity, generations, replay/rollback and authorization remain separate. Ciphertext record storage needs a demonstrated completeness/rollback strategy and a search leakage budget. [Zetetic design](https://www.zetetic.net/sqlcipher/design/)
9. **What if the key is unavailable?** Enter locked/unavailable with a typed reason, preserve files, deny reads/writes and publication, preserve recovery/deletion knowledge and offer authorized retry or offline recovery when appropriate. Missing, denied, entitlement failure, wrong key and corruption must remain distinguishable operationally without exposing secrets. Current native create/delete `-34018` and missing-item `-25300` do not prove unlock. [Keychain blocker](../WS-F2R-owner-handoff/signing-keychain-blockers.md)
10. **Can the app fail closed without opening or modifying data?** The protected composition currently refuses access and can avoid an open entirely. Future workers must prove the same on missing/rejected authority, path/generation mismatch and pre-auth failures. D2A-01 is a separate constraint for valid keys opening existing SQLCipher files. Refusal is a safe state, not reopen functionality. [Catalog stub](../../../../../qualification/webkit-storage-f0/Sources/BrowserPersistence/ProtectedCatalog.swift), [D2A-01 analysis](d2a-01-impact-analysis.md)
11. **Can deletion and recovery meet OD-R01–20 without SQLCipher?** In principle through scoped authenticated storage, portable recovery, durable deletion/reconciliation and independent engine lifecycle. None of the alternatives has qualified the full chain. Stock SQLite alone fails full profile encryption. Destroying a key or removing a store cannot prove secure erasure across snapshots or old independent backups. [Owner matrix](owner-decision-traceability.md), [backup design](backup-recovery-migration.md)
12. **What does retaining it cost?** Pin and maintain provider source/build/ABI, verify vendor provenance and security updates, isolate symbols/worker, qualify the broker VFS and open/read/write fence, profile per-connection derivation and paging, verify sidecars, and design recovery/version upgrades. It retains proven database abstractions and shifts page cryptography away from application code. Keychain, release identity, backups, policy, restore and lock work remain regardless of backend. [Comparison](architecture-options.md), [cost plan](performance-maintainability.md)

## Open, write, migration and corruption

At this pin `sqlite3_open_v2` constructs the pager before the handle-based length-explicit key call. Key receipt, binding, deferred derivation, page authentication and authenticated product identity are distinct. Preserve read scope and original-file mutation fences; authenticated schema/suite/profile/store/key-version identity and current epoch are prerequisites for readiness and a separate write grant. Never derive authorization from an error that looks like “not a database.” Preserve originals and sidecars for authorized recovery, with no migration, checkpoint or automatic recreate on key or identity failure. New exclusive create is a separate path. [D2A-01](d2a-01-impact-analysis.md), [OPEN-02](../M1/m1-sqlcipher-open02-write-fence-specification.md), [OPEN-03](../M1/m1-sqlcipher-open03-vfs-confinement-specification.md), [OPEN-04/05](../M1/m1-sqlcipher-open04-open05-protocol-and-recovery-specification.md)

WAL, journals, shared-memory structures, temporary sort/index and diagnostic files need build- and configuration-specific leakage tests. Vendor design discusses encrypted page data including journals; filesystem metadata and native sidecar contents still need the agreed bounded oracle. Never equate source configuration or main-file ciphertext with complete runtime coverage. The current app has no real provider migration/recovery executor.
