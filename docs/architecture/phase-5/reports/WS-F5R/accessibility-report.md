# VoiceOver and accessibility

WS-F5 observed native AX roles and keyboard behavior. Its VoiceOver attempt remains INCONCLUSIVE because speech or captions could not be observed. [WS-F5 evidence](../WS-F5/accessibility-report.md)

Use an operator and the macOS VoiceOver caption panel or observable speech for VO-01 through VO-08. Capture the selected item, its announced name, role and state, the resulting action, and the focus transition where applicable. Record AX-01 independently. Restore the operator's prior VoiceOver and caption settings at the end of the session. [Apple VoiceOver caption panel guide](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/voiceover/unac078/mac)
