# Feature opportunity matrix

Ratings describe implementation effort and residual security/reliability exposure for the proposed scope. Rank applies to the five opportunities after WS-F5R. Category does not confer implementation authorization. The detailed [recommendation cards](architecture-recommendations.md) cover every ADOPT and ADAPT item.

| ID and opportunity | Vezham baseline | Inspiration | Expected user value | Complexity | Security / reliability risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Stable workspace graph | Window-local state tied to `ViewID`; no restart model | Search saved rows, Zen session manager | High, recover project organization and avoid losing context | Large | High if persisted before custody; model-only scope lower | **ADOPT** after WS-F5R, qualify with synthetic state in WS-F6 |
| 2 · Lazy view admission | Eager view per tab and per recovered slot | Search, Vela | High, immediate organization with lower potential startup work | Large | High, concurrent activation and store assignment | **ADAPT** in WS-F6, measure before and after |
| 3 · Scoped commands and keyboard equivalents | Existing search and action bar, menu and context actions | Arc, SigmaOS, Flecha | High, easier everyday switching and manipulation | Small | Medium, focus, input handling and cross-context confusion | **ADOPT** after WS-F5R |
| 4 · Explicit Mini destination and handoff | URL-only handoff to owning main controller, accepted dispatch | Little Arc, Cra Little Windows | High, keep a useful link in the intended project | Medium | High, cross-scope navigation and disclosure | **ADAPT** after routing qualification, use WS-F6 destination identity |
| 5 · Restore two-pane layout intent | Two native panes and compact fallback, session-scoped | Search, SigmaOS, Zen | High, recover research/authoring pairs and focus | Medium | Medium, stale member references and misdirected actions | **ADAPT** in WS-F6 model |
| 6 · Live-tab discard and keep-awake | Attached only when visible, views still owned while hidden | Search, SigmaOS, Vela | Potentially high with large workspaces | Large | High, unsaved input/media/capture/requests | **DEFER** until lazy baseline and measured pressure justify it |
| 7 · Metadata-only import/export preview | No workspace importer | Arc export, Stedding, Cra, Vela | Medium-high, migrate and leave with links intact | Medium | Medium-high, hostile links/files, accidental disclosure | **INVESTIGATE** explicit file selection and reversible preview after graph contract |
| 8 · Compact sidebar/reveal | Existing sidebar and narrow split behavior | Zen Compact Mode, Cra | Medium, more reading room | Medium | Medium, focus and privacy indicator discovery | **INVESTIGATE** bounded accessibility study |
| 9 · Inline Peek/Glance | Separate Mini, no overlay | Zen, Arc, Stedding, Vela | Medium, quick look without switching | Medium | High, origin and scope ambiguity; hidden view cost | **DEFER** until Mini and split workflows are qualified |
| 10 · Nested folders / separate Favorites tier | Flat groups and pins | Arc, Zen, Stedding | Uncertain beyond power users | Medium | Medium, hierarchy, ordering and migration | **DEFER** until evidence shows demand after WS-F6 |
| 11 · Done/Snooze/automatic archive | Manual close and pin | SigmaOS and Arc Today | Uncertain, reduces clutter for some users | Medium | Medium-high, accidental removal, retention and notifications | **INVESTIGATE** semantics and reversible manual actions first |
| 12 · Declarative content filter | No product filter located | Search, Refrax, Stedding | Medium-high if compatibility holds | Medium | Medium-high, breakage and list supply chain | **INVESTIGATE** public WebKit fixture after foundation |
| 13 · Signed protected profiles and owned backup | Gates, adapters and synthetic archive; no product path | Search/Refrax as contrasts, existing Vezham ADRs | High, trusted durability and recovery | Large | High, keys/custody, rollback and WebKit boundaries | **DEFER** product exposure to WS-F7 qualification |
| 14 · Web Inspector development opt-in | Isolated native harness and stand-ins | Flecha DevTools, Apple APIs | Medium, faster diagnosis | Small | Medium, inspecting sensitive pages | **INVESTIGATE** dev-only with explicit fixture scope |
| 15 · Bounded WebKit extension pilot | No product extension runtime | Search, Refrax | Uncertain pending named extension requirements | Large | High, data-store and host permissions | **DEFER** until profile/release foundations and approved use cases |
| 16 · Local-only test automation | Debug fixture modes already exist | Search bench-MCP, Refrax caller policy | Medium, improves reproducibility | Medium | High if connected to real user sessions | **INVESTIGATE** disposable fixture-only scope after qualification |
| 17 · Dual engines, CEF, Gecko or Electron runtime | Native host and WebKit direction | Cra, Refrax, Zen, Vela | Compatibility gains are workload-dependent | Large | High, duplicated trust/patching/packaging | **REJECT** |
| 18 · Chrome Store parity, native messaging or Boosts as launch feature | No extension/injection product surface | Search, Flecha, Vela, Zen Mods | Uncertain without specific jobs | Large | High, code execution and cross-scope data | **REJECT** as launch requirements |
| 19 · Broad product MCP/CDP, CLI scripting or agent access | Bounded debug runners only | Flecha, Refrax | High for a niche, limited for core users | Large | High, action/credential/privacy authority | **REJECT** broad control surface |
| 20 · Cookie/password migration, private disk restore, or cross-engine store cloning | Private-only browsing and fail-closed profile | Chromium-derived importers; Arc archive is a retention contrast | Convenience comes with major trust cost | Large | High, secret exposure and unsafe retention | **REJECT** |
| 21 · Mandatory cloud sync or remote browser services | Local-first foundations | SigmaOS sync and Stedding file sync | Uncertain for current scope | Large | High, multi-writer, custody and availability | **REJECT** as foundation/release requirements |
| 22 · First-class download workflow | Unsupported MIME denied; successful flow open | Search's WebKit delegate, Cra/Vela UX | High for browser viability | Medium | High, file destination and lifecycle | **DEFER** product scope to existing qualification/design gates; resolve WS-F5R feasibility first |
